Ri ruk'amonik re' kuk'ut ronojel ri k'atz'in chawech je' ajpatan: tak'ij, tak'ul siponik, tatak b'aq' chike taq tzijob'äl, tatz'ib'aj taq ch'ab'äl, chuqa' ch'ab'ej ri tzijtz'ib'axik.

## Tak'ul ri aretalib'al

Ri ak'amalb'e kuya' chawech jujun:

- Jun **nsec** (WebSocket etz'apwach clave) — jun tzij ri tik'otob' ruk' `nsec1`
- Jun **sipan b'ey** — jun URL xwi jun b'ey ri kuk'otob' aretalib'al chawech

**Tachajij ri ansec.** Ri are' aretz'ib' chuqa' atak'ij. Jachin k'oj ri unsec tikowin kuk'oj awib' je' rat. Taya' pa jun chajinik tzij.

## Tak'ij

1. Tajaq ri ruch'awib'al runik'oj pa anuk'samaj chib'äl
2. Tatz'ib'aj ri `nsec` pa ri nuk'samaj wokisaxik
3. Ri runik'oj kach'ob' ri achi'el ruk' etz'apwach — ri etz'apwach clave man tiel ta pa anuk'samaj chib'äl

Chrij ri nab'ej tak'ij, kak'ut chaw chike taya' ri ab'ij chuqa' ri ach'ab'äl.

### Tak'ij ruk' passkey (we nawaj)

We ri ak'amalb'e xuya' b'ey chike passkey, takowin tatz'ib'aj jun ch'akul clave o chi pa **Runik'oj**. Re' kuya' chawech tatak'ij pa taq chapb'äl chik majun uch'ab'exik ri nsec.

## Ri k'utb'al

Toq xatak'ij, kawokisaj ri k'utb'al ruk':

- **Elq'aj taq siponik** — taq siponik ri e b'an chuwach
- **Ri ach'akul ri' rajal** — k'ut pa ruch'i b'ey (ch'akul ri' pa q'otij o ri kapeq)
- **Ajpatanib' pa k'ayb'al** — ja'nik ri e k'oj

## Tak'ul siponik

Toq jun siponik kapon pa ri ach'akul ri', kawokolmaj ruk':

- Jun **ch'ab'äl siponik** pa ri nuk'samaj chib'äl (tajaq/tachup pa Runik'oj)
- Jun **tzijol okisan** we xaya' b'ey
- Jun **k'ak' b'ij pa ri k'utb'al chib'äl**

Tatz'aq pa **Tak'ul** chike ri tachap ri siponik. Ri ateléfono chab'äl — tak'ul chike tak'ay ri' ruk' ri ajoyon. We jun ajpatan chik nuchap nab'ej, ri uxojik ch'ab'äl tich'op.

## Pa jun siponik

Toq k'o pa jun siponik, katik'tik' ruk':

- Jun **q'otij siponik** ri kuk'ut ja'nik ri siponik
- Jun **ruxak tz'ib'axik** jawi' takowin tatz'ib'aj taq ch'ab'äl pa ri q'otij ri'
- Jun **tijoj tz'ilan tzij** b'oton chike ri tatz'ijaj ri ajoyon

Ri tz'ib'axik e chajin ruk' ti'j etz'apwach. Takowin tayak ri tz'ib'axik ruk' aq'ab'.

## Tatz'ib'aj taq ch'ab'äl

Ri tz'ib'axik e tz'aq etz'apwach pa anuk'samaj chib'äl chi k'a chwech e tak'ay pa ri ajk'ayb'al. Xwi rat chuqa' ri k'amalb'e kikowin kik'ay.

We ri ak'amalb'e xuya' taq ruk'utb'al etz'apwach (tzij, tacha', retal, etc.), e k'ut pa ri ch'aloj tz'ib'axik. Tachapij je' ri k'atz'in — e etz'apwach ruk' ri atzij tz'ib'axik.

Tab'e pa **Tz'ib'axik** pa ruch'i b'ey chike tak'ut, tanik'aj, o takanoj ri etz'apwach atz'ib'axik. Takowin taya' ri atz'ib'axik je' jun wuj etz'apwach.

## Tzijtz'ib'axik

We ri tzijtz'ib'axik tijaq (ruk' ri k'amalb'e chuqa' ruk' ri awokisaxik), ri siponik e tz'ib'ax wi toq ketani' chik. Ri tzijtz'ib'axik k'ut ruk' ri atz'ib'axik chi kij ri siponik.

Takowin tajaq/tachup ri tzijtz'ib'axik pa **Runik'oj**. Toq ch'uptajik, ri asiponik man ketz'ib'ax ta chik.

Ri tzijtz'ib'axik e etz'apwach pa ri yakb'al — ri ajk'ayb'al kach'aj ri ch'ab'äl pa q'otij, tek'uri' titz'apij ri tzij.

## Ch'awib'al

We ri ak'amalb'e xuya' b'ey chike b'eyab' tzijob'exik (SMS, WhatsApp, o Signal), katik'tik' jun **Ch'awib'al** sipan pa ruch'i b'ey. Ri re' kuk'ut taq ch'awib'al ruk' tzijob'äl rech winak ri xkik'ay k'ayb'al ruk' ri ruch'awib'al ruk' tzij.

Chi jujun ch'awib'al kuk'ut:
- T'ujul taq tzijob'äl ruk' retalib'al q'otij ri k'ut jachin xuya'
- Ri b'eyal ri xapon wi ri tzijob'äl (SMS, WhatsApp, Signal)
- K'ak' taq tzijob'äl e k'exoj pa ri q'otij ri'

Chike ri tatak b'aq', tatz'ib'aj ri atzijob'äl pa ri ch'aloj b'aq' chi k'aj ri ch'awib'al. Ri ab'aq' tak'ay pa ri b'eyal ri xuk'ay ri winak chike ri xkich'aw awuk'.

## Tak'oj awib' pa q'uq'j

Tachap ri **q'uq'j** pa ruch'i b'ey chike tach'op ri taq siponik ri e k'ayew pa ach'akul ri'. Ri taq siponik man kekisipon ta pa achateléfono toq k'o pa q'uq'j. Tachap jumul chik toq katzolin.

## Taq Na'oj

- Tach'ab'ej <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (o <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> pa Mac) chike tajaq ri wokisaxik chib'äl che ri k'ayew b'ey.
- Tatz'aq pa <kbd>?</kbd> chike tak'ut ronojel taq kib'ey chawib'al.
- Tikojo ri runik'oj je' PWA chike jun k'ojik runik'oj chuqa' utz taq tzijol.
- Tachajij ri anuk'samaj chib'äl jaq pa ri ach'akul ri' chike taq tzijol siponik pa ri q'otij ri'.
- Tach'ab'ej ri **Toq'ob'** ruxak (pa ruch'i b'ey o wokisaxik chib'äl) chike FAQ, ruk'amonik, chuqa' taq kib'ey chawib'al.
