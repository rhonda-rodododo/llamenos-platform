**Ruk'isanem rutz'akuxik: May 4, 2026**

Llámenos xtz'uk pa jun EU-based k'ayib'äl. Pa GDPR Article 17, awetaman chi awe richin nitaq ruyuj aw personal tzij. Re' ruxaq' nuk'ut chawe achike rub'eyal nub'än re' chuqa' achike tzij niyuj o niyak.

---

## Achike Rub'eyal Nitaq Ruyuj Tzij

Yatikïr nitaq ruyuj aw account chuqa' konojel associated tzij through any of ri k'utun taq b'ey:

### 1. In-app (self-service)

Katb'e pa **Settings → Account → Delete Account** within ri Llámenos app. Re' immediately nuyuj aw account chuqa' konojel associated tzij pa aw hub.

### 2. Via aw hub administrator

Hub administrators yetikïr nik'ul volunteer accounts chuqa' konojel associated tzij pa ri admin panel. Tach'utiwach ri administrator aw hub chuqa' tach'utiwach chi re' chi nuyuj aw account.

### 3. Pa email

Titaq jun ruyuj pa [privacy@llamenos-platform.com](mailto:privacy@llamenos-platform.com) rik'in ri k'utun taq tzij:

- Aw display name pa ri app
- Ri b'i'aj aw hub (achike, ri k'ayib'äl ri nusamaj ri hotline)

Taqsamaj nik'oje' within **30 q'ij** per GDPR Article 17.

## Achike Niyuj

We aw account xiyuj, ri k'utun taq tzij permanently xiyuj:

| Tzij | Xiyuj? |
|------|----------|
| Account taq ewan taq tzij chuqa' device keys | Yes — immediately |
| Display name chuqa' role | Yes — immediately |
| Konojel encrypted notes chuqa' reports xatz'ib'aj | Yes — ciphertext xiyuj pa ruk'u'x samaj |
| Case records chuqa' contact records xatz'uk | Yes — ciphertext xiyuj pa ruk'u'x samaj |
| Push notification token (FCM) | Yes — immediately |
| Shift records chuqa' availability tzij | Yes — immediately |
| Crash reports associated rik'in aw account | Yes — within 30 q'ij |

## Achike Niyak (chuqa' Achike)

Jujun tzij niyak chuwäch ruyuj richin legal chuqa' integrity reasons:

### Audit log entries — anonymized, retained indefinitely

Llámenos nrokisaj jun tamper-evident hash-chained audit log richin detect unauthorized modifications pa activity records. Ruyuj individual entries xub'än ri chain chuqa' undermine ri integrity guarantee.

**Achike nub'án instead:** Konojel personal identifiers pa audit log entries ri nuya' rutzijol chawe (aw display name, account ID) e replaced rik'in jun anonymized hash. Ri event itself (achike, "tzij xk'ul pa 14:32") niyak, pero man yatikïr ta nokem chik chawe chuwäch anonymization.

**Rutz'akuxik peraj:** Lifetime aw hub. Ri hub administrator nuch'ajin we ri hub's audit log xiyuj.

### References pa ch'aqa' chik users' content — anonymized

We jun chik user's encrypted notes nuya' rutzijol chawe achi'el jun tzij participant, re' reference anonymized (replaced rik'in jun placeholder). Ri chik user's content man xiyuj ta — ruk'u'x samaj ruk'u'x samaj.
